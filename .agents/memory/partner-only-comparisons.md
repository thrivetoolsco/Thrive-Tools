---
name: Partner-only comparisons
description: Editorial rule for missing affiliate offers in comparison and gift-guide posts.
---

When a comparison or gift-guide brief names a brand with no affiliate link or code in the live discount directory, omit that brand and adjust the article's counts, recommendations, and FAQs rather than publishing it as a shoppable partner.

**Why:** The site owner chose omission for brands that lacked their affiliate links or codes and said not to share posts promoting those unlinked brands.

**How to apply:** Confirm the directory's source offers before composing partner-based buying guides. Link-only entries may be used as links, but do not invent a code or claim automatic savings when the source does not state that. Keep work save-only until the owner reviews and publishes it.
