---
name: Blog article ordering
description: Owner's standing rule for ordering newly shared blog articles.
---

The owner says: "The last articles I share should always appear first in the blog page."

**Why:** Newly supplied articles must be easy to find rather than appearing behind older content in the directory.

**How to apply:** Keep the blog newest-first using the article's original date, not an offer review date or an edit timestamp. Place new registry records before existing same-day records so the newest shared post wins ties. Preserve the article's supplied date; if a newly shared backdated article would otherwise be buried, track its addition date separately for listing order rather than falsifying the article date.
