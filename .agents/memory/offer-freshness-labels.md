---
name: Offer freshness labels
description: How to date discount-code pages without implying unperformed verification.
---

Keep discount-code "Active [Month Year]" labels fixed to the latest reviewed month rather than computing them automatically from the current date.

**Why:** Automatically advancing a freshness label would assert that affiliate codes were checked in a new month even when nobody reviewed them. A stale but honest date is safer than a fresh-looking unverified claim.

**How to apply:** When building or updating discount-code roundups, change the active month only as part of an actual offer review. Avoid a client-side date formatter for offer validity.
