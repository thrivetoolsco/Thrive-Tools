---
name: Catalog counts over old briefs
description: Resolving a mismatch between a fixed brief count and the current discount directory.
---

When a brief cites a fixed number of brands but the live discount directory has grown, include every current card in structured data and use the live count in aggregate answers. Do not cut valid offers to meet an obsolete number.

**Why:** The pre-publish brief cited 40 brands, but the directory contained 43 after its requested additions. Omitting three would make the structured data incomplete; repeating “40” in the Q&A hub would be inaccurate.

**How to apply:** Recount the directory when changing offer lists, FAQ hubs, or aggregate claims. Prefer deriving counts from source data where practical, and call out any divergence from the brief during review.
