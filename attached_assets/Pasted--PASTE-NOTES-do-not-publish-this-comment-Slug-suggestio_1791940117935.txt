## The 12 hallmarks of aging, in plain terms

The "hallmarks of aging" are a framework from aging research: a set of biological processes that, together, drive how and why our bodies age at the cellular level. Scientists group them into twelve — things like cells losing their ability to divide and renew, mitochondria (the cell's energy producers) becoming less efficient, waste proteins accumulating faster than the cell can clear them, chronic low-grade inflammation rising, and the gut microbiome drifting out of balance.

Two things are worth knowing about the framework. First, the hallmarks are connected: when one worsens, it tends to pull others with it, which is why aging researchers increasingly think in terms of systems rather than single causes. Second, several hallmarks — cellular energy production and autophagy among them — respond to lifestyle and nutritional inputs, which is exactly the territory a formula like this is built for.

When IM8 says Longevity targets all 12 hallmarks, that is the brand's own positioning for its 10-compound formula. It is a useful way to understand what the product is *designed* to do — and, as with any supplement, it is a claim about nutritional support, not a promise that a drink reverses aging.
