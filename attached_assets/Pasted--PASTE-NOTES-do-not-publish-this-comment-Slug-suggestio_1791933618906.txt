## What is Zentein?

Zentein Nutrition is a Canadian food brand based in Ontario, making high-protein snacks and collagen powders since 2020 under the tagline "Eat, Feel, and Perform Better." Its whole positioning rests on short, readable labels: Zentein states its products are made **without artificial sweeteners, colours or preservatives**. Two practical details stand out. First, Zentein says its products are **made fresh weekly** in Ontario rather than sitting in a warehouse for months. Second, the products are shelf-stable, so they're easy to keep at work or in a gym bag. Zentein displays a **4.95/5 rating across 1,348 customer reviews** (drawn from Google, Shop, and its own website) on zentein.ca — a strong displayed score for a brand built largely on repeat customers and word of mouth.
