## What Is the Glycine in Beef Protein Good For?

Glycine is a conditionally essential amino acid present in high concentrations in beef protein — particularly from grass-fed beef and bones. It is one of the most underappreciated nutrients in modern diets because the shift away from nose-to-tail eating has dramatically reduced glycine intake.

**What glycine does:**
- Supports collagen synthesis — the primary structural protein in skin, joints, tendons, and connective tissue
- Supports gut barrier integrity — glycine is required for maintaining tight junctions in the intestinal epithelium
- Supports sleep quality — glycine acts as an inhibitory neurotransmitter and has been shown in clinical trials to improve sleep onset and quality at 3g doses
- Supports liver detoxification — glycine conjugates with bile acids and toxic compounds for excretion
- Anti-inflammatory — glycine inhibits the release of inflammatory cytokines from macrophages

Most protein powders provide minimal glycine. Equip Prime Protein delivers approximately 5g per serving — making it a meaningful glycine source alongside its complete amino acid profile.
